About the church

A story of grace and humble beginnings.

A family of believers in Lusaka, learning to follow Jesus together.

Who we are

Rooted in prayer. Shaped by faith.

Souls for Christ Ministries Zambia is a Spirit-filled church family in Lusaka. We gather to worship God, grow in His Word, pray with faith, and reach our community with the love of Jesus.

Our heart is simple: to win souls for Christ and help every believer grow into the fullness of what God has called them to be.

Our journey

From a prayer to a family.

  • A prayer begins

    A small gathering begins to seek God for their community.

  • A church family forms

    The gathering grows into a church family with a heart for prayer, the Word, and soul winning.

  • A wider family

    The ministry is shaped by the counsel and fellowship of spiritual leaders God has placed around it.

  • Today

    Morning Glory, midweek services, Sunday worship, choirs, prayer, and outreach continue to reach souls for Christ.

  • Tomorrow

    We are believing God for a permanent home and a stronger branch in Chongwe.

Our founder

Prophetess Mulenga Chilufya Mutampuka.

Founder and Spiritual Leader

Her ministry is marked by prayer, prophetic encouragement, deliverance, and a deep love for people. Under her leadership, the church has become a place where the broken find hope and families are strengthened.

She serves alongside her blood sister, Prophetess Mubanga Mutampuka, and values the counsel of the spiritual family and leaders God has placed around the ministry.

Our mission

To win souls for Christ, teach the Word of God, and help every believer grow in prayer, faith, and service.

Our vision

To be a Spirit-filled church where every soul is reached, every family is strengthened, and every believer is equipped for the work of the ministry.
