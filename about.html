Who we are

A story of grace, faith, and humble beginnings

Souls for Christ Ministries Zambia is a Christian ministry based in Lusaka, Zambia. We are a family of believers who love God, honour His Word, and believe in the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our heart is to win souls for Christ, disciple believers, and serve our community with compassion. We gather for Sunday celebration, Tuesday service, prayer and intercession, and times of worship led by our choirs and praise team.

The ministry’s story is one of humble beginnings and God’s faithfulness. What started as a small gathering of praying hearts has grown into a church family with a clear mission and a growing branch in Chongwe.

A prayer begins

A small gathering of believers begins to pray, worship, and seek God for the souls of their community.

A church family forms

The gathering grows into a church family in Lusaka, with a heart for prayer, the Word, and soul winning.

A wider spiritual family

The ministry is shaped by the counsel and fellowship of spiritual leaders God has placed around it.

Today

Sunday and Tuesday services, choirs, prayer, outreach, and community care continue to reach souls for Christ.

Tomorrow

We are believing God for a permanent home, a stronger branch in Chongwe, and many more souls won for the Kingdom.

Our founder

Prophetess Mulenga Chilufya Mutampuka

Founder and Spiritual Leader

Prophetess Mulenga Chilufya Mutampuka is the founder and spiritual leader of Souls for Christ Ministries Zambia. Her ministry is marked by prayer, prophetic encouragement, deliverance, and a deep love for people.

Under her leadership, the church has become a place where the broken find hope, families are strengthened, and believers are equipped to win souls for Christ. She serves alongside her blood sister, Prophetess Mubanga Mutampuka, and values the counsel of the spiritual family and leaders God has placed around the ministry.

Her heart is simple: to see every soul reached, every believer growing, and every family walking in the love and power of Jesus Christ.

Our statement of faith
Family and faith

A ministry built on family

The call of God is lived out in the everyday places: in marriage, in motherhood, in family, and in the church family. Prophetess Chilufya’s life and ministry are a testimony of faith carried through family.

We honour the family and spiritual family that stand with this house. Their love, prayers, and support make room for the work of the Gospel.
