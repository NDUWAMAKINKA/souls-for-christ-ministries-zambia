Take a moment to breathe, read, and pray. God’s Word is a lamp to your feet and a light to your path.

Today

God has not forgotten you

“For the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost.”Luke 19:10

Whatever you are carrying today, bring it to God in prayer. He sees you, He knows your name, and He has not forgotten you. His Word is a lamp to your feet and a light to your path.

Stay rooted in Scripture. Keep your heart open to the Holy Spirit. Trust that God is working even when you cannot see the whole picture. His timing is perfect, and His love for you is sure.

Today, choose faith over fear. Choose obedience over convenience. Choose to believe that the same power that raised Christ from the dead is at work in you.

Prophetess Mulenga Chilufya Mutampuka
