Take a moment to breathe, read, and pray.

TodayA moment with God
TodayDaily devotional

God has not forgotten you.

Whatever you are carrying today, bring it to God in prayer. He sees you, He knows your name, and He has not forgotten you.

There are seasons when the answer feels slow and the road feels long. In those moments, faith is not pretending that everything is easy. Faith is choosing to trust that God is still present, still working, and still faithful to His promises.

“For the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost.”Luke 19:10

You are not outside of His reach. The same Jesus who sought the lost is still drawing near to you today. Bring Him your fear, your waiting, and your questions. He is not distant from your story.

A prayer for today: Lord, thank You that I am seen and known by You. Help me to trust Your timing and to walk with You in this season. Give me peace where I feel anxious, courage where I feel tired, and a fresh awareness of Your presence. Amen.

01

Sit with the Word

Read Luke 19:1–10 and notice how Jesus moved toward the one who felt overlooked.

02

Name the need

Tell God honestly what you are carrying. You do not have to tidy your prayer before you bring it.

03

Give thanks

Before the day ends, name one evidence of God's faithfulness, however small.
