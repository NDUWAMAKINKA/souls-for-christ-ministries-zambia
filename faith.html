Statement of faith

What we believe.

The foundations that shape our worship and life together.

The Bible

The inspired, infallible Word of God and our final authority for faith and life.

God

One God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

Fully God and fully man. He died for our sins, rose again, and is coming back for His church.

The Holy Spirit

He convicts, comforts, gifts, guides, and empowers believers for witness and service.

Salvation

By grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone, not by works, and followed by a new life of obedience.

The Church

The body of Christ, called to worship, fellowship, disciple believers, and carry the Gospel to the nations.

Souls for ChristMinistries Zambia

Winning souls to Christ.
