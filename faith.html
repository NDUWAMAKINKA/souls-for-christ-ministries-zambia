Statement of faith

The foundations of our faith

We affirm the historic Christian faith and joyfully submit to the authority of Scripture. These are the truths that shape our worship, our teaching, and our life together.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and our final authority for faith and life.

2 Timothy 3:16–17

God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Deuteronomy 6:4 · Matthew 28:19

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man. He died for our sins, rose again, and is coming back for His church.

John 1:14 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–4

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the person and power of the Holy Spirit, who convicts, comforts, gifts, guides, and empowers believers for witness and service.

John 14:16–17 · Acts 1:8

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone, not by works, and that every believer is called to a new life of obedience.

Ephesians 2:8–10

The Church

We believe the church is the body of Christ, called to worship, fellowship, disciple believers, and carry the Gospel to the nations.

Acts 2:42–47 · Matthew 28:18–20

Prayer & prophecy

We believe God hears and answers prayer, and that the Holy Spirit still speaks today to encourage, strengthen, and direct His people.

Jeremiah 33:3 · 1 Corinthians 14:3

Soul winning

We believe every believer is called to share the love of Jesus and to seek the lost, because every soul matters to God.

Luke 19:10 · Mark 16:15
Our confession

Christ at the centre of everything

We are not built on a personality or a denomination. We are built on Jesus Christ, the Word of God, and the power of the Holy Spirit. Our spiritual heritage points us back to Him.
