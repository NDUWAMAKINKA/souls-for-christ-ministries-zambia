Give with a cheerful heart.

Your giving supports the Gospel, the church family, and care for our community.

Tithes & offeringsLusaka, Zambia

Ways to give

Generosity with joy.

Every gift is an act of worship. Give securely through the church office, or in person at any service.

Give in person

Tithes and offerings can be given during any service in Lusaka. Our team will help you if you are visiting for the first time.

Mobile money

Call or WhatsApp the church office to arrange a mobile-money gift. We will confirm the correct details with you directly.

Message the office ↗

Bank transfer

For verified bank details, contact the church office directly. We will share the correct information and help you complete your gift.

Contact us ↗

For your safety, always confirm giving details directly with the church office. We will never ask for your PIN or password.

Where your giving goes

Stand with the work.

Partnership is more than a gift. It is standing with the church to reach souls and strengthen families.

Reach souls

Help share the love of Jesus and disciple new believers.

Care for families

Support welfare, weddings, funerals, and families in hard seasons.

Build the house

Help strengthen the church, its ministries, and the Chongwe branch.

Serve the city

Make room for practical love and outreach in our community.

A promise to carry

“Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.”2 Corinthians 9:7

Thank you for partnering with what God is doing in Lusaka and Chongwe.
