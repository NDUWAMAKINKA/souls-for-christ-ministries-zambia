AGCOM

Ark of God Covenant Ministry

Spiritual covering · Apostle John Chi

Ark of God Covenant Ministry (AGCOM) was founded by Apostle John Chi under the instruction of the Holy Spirit. From humble beginnings in Buea, Cameroon, the ministry has grown into a global movement with a mandate of prophecy, power, and solution.

Apostle John Chi is a minister mentored by Prophet TB Joshua. Prophetess Chilufya has publicly celebrated the grace upon his life and the ministry’s impact in Zambia, including the Zambia for Christ crusade in Lusaka.

Visit AGCOM TV
SCOAN

The Synagogue, Church Of All Nations

Spiritual root · Prophet TB Joshua legacy

The Synagogue, Church Of All Nations (SCOAN) in Ikotun-Egbe, Lagos, is a globally recognised ministry founded by Prophet TB Joshua. Its Emmanuel TV broadcast has carried the Gospel, healing, and deliverance to nations around the world.

Today, SCOAN is led by Pastor Evelyn Joshua. We honour the enduring legacy of faith, prayer, and the power of the Holy Spirit that flows through this spiritual heritage.

Visit SCOAN
Why this matters

A heritage of prayer, power, and souls

We are not isolated. We belong to a wider body of Christ, and we are grateful for the spiritual fathers, mothers, and leaders who have prayed for this house and spoken into its life.

From this heritage we receive a simple commission: preach the Gospel, pray for the sick, encourage the broken, and win souls for Christ. We honour the lineage, but our confidence is in Jesus alone.

Our prayer is that the same grace upon AGCOM and SCOAN will continue to rest upon Souls for Christ Ministries Zambia and upon every soul we reach.
