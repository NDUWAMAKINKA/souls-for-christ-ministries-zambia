A place to serve and grow

God has given every member a gift. Our ministries work together to serve the church, reach the community, and make room for every person to grow.

Main Choir

Leading the congregation in worship and ministering through song at Sunday and special services.

Revelation Choir

A second choir raising a sound of worship and encouraging the church through music.

Praise Team

Leading the church into the presence of God with songs of praise and thanksgiving.

Prayer & Intercession

Standing in the gap for the church, the nation, and those in need through consistent prayer.

Soul Winning & Outreach

Taking the love of Christ beyond the church walls into Chawama and surrounding communities.

Ushers & Hospitality

Welcoming every visitor, keeping order, and making everyone feel at home.

Friends’ Committee

Building friendship, care, and support among members and new believers.

Mothers’ Committee

Caring for families, encouraging mothers, and supporting the church’s welfare work.

Church Committee

Serving the church through administration, planning, and practical support.
