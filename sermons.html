Sermon outline

Your situation is not bigger than God

Key Scriptures: Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:17; Ephesians 3:20.

What you are facing is real. It may be a health report, a family struggle, a financial pressure, or a door that has stayed closed. But the message of the house is that your situation is not bigger than God. He is the God who speaks peace to storms, opens doors no one can shut, and makes a way where there seems to be no way.

  1. Name the situation honestly. Faith is not pretending everything is fine. Bring the real thing to the real God.
  2. Remember who God is. Nothing is impossible with God. He is the Creator, the Healer, the Provider, and the Faithful Father.
  3. Speak faith, not fear. Let your words agree with God’s Word. Death and life are in the power of the tongue.
  4. Praise God before the answer comes. Worship is a weapon. It lifts your eyes from the problem to the Promise Keeper.
  5. Stand with the church in prayer. You are not alone. The church family is here to pray with you and stand with you.

Prayer: Father, I bring my situation to You. I choose to trust You more than I fear what I see. Strengthen my faith, fill me with Your peace, and let Your will be done in my life. In Jesus’ name, amen.
